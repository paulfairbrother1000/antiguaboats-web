# Antigua Boats pay-per-seat and special offers

Approved in conversation on 9 October 2026. Preserve existing design and existing private/two-party shared products.

## Product and commercial rules
New product: Full Day Charter – Pay per seat, 10:00–17:00 Antigua local time. Vehicle configuration holds target revenue 180000 cents, contribution cap 20000 cents, minimum 6 paid seats, maximum surcharge 10000 cents per seat, promotion 20%, and capacity (initially 8, matching the existing shared charter). Seat price = ceiling(target/minimum), cents. Snapshot vehicle settings on trip creation and ticket price/consent on booking. Follow the existing mock booking flow for this phase, explicitly recorded as PAID_MOCK. Stripe integration for the whole site is deferred by the user. Holds do not count. Six completed bookings’ seats confirms trip with customer/operator emails. At T-24, use actual paid original ticket revenue; subtract up to contribution cap from revenue shortfall, divide remainder across paid seats, distribute integer cents without exceeding cap. Three or fewer initial seats cancels/refunds; generalized feasibility is derived from min seats, revenue and caps, rather than hard-coded four/five. Failure to collect a surcharge remains operationally visible and does not claim payment success.

## Promotions
Only after paid minimum is met AND between T-72 and T-24, discount remaining seats by configurable 20%. Preserve previous ticket prices. No promotion on below-minimum trips. Public offer API and page expose only seats actually available, never held, cancelled, expired or customer data. Pace discount seats only in same window, using server-verified discounted inventory from partner API, without guessing based on route prices.

## Customer pages
Group charter tiles into Private Charters and Shared Charters using supplied copy. Product page uses existing CharterTemplate, full-day hero/images. Replace preference-choice language for new product with mandatory surcharge consent and full-refund terms. Main booking calendar shades occupied pay-per-seat days purple, black when full. New per-seat booking flow shows live sold, needed, available seats and quote, mandatory cap acceptance, server-created payment checkout. /specialoffers sections: active special offers, shared charters (one remaining party of up to four), pay-per-seat trips, discounted shuttles. Only real upcoming inventory; no example dates/prices. Link in navigation and homepage.

## Backend and operations
Dedicated trip/seat-booking tables with RLS and server-only writes. Serialize reservations and legacy charter conflicts by date/vehicle lock; database checks both directions. Atomic mock booking completion counts booked seats once and records surcharge consent. Refund/charge/email outbox uses stable idempotency keys and claims; scheduler does decisions independently of page views. Keep money, booking records and external payment confirmations separate. Use existing mock payments, per explicit user instruction. Outbound email uses Resend and operator address settings. Mail sender/operator and delivery credentials are required for actual outgoing emails; queue pending deliveries transparently. Social content feed is delivered; external social publishing requires authorized connected accounts/group access and is not silently simulated.
